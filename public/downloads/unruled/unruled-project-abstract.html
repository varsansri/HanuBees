Project abstract

Browser strategy: semantic content is readable without animation. Backdrop blur, pointer lighting, scrolling tickers, and observer reveals are progressive enhancements. The reduced-motion preference collapses non-essential movement. Desktop and 390-pixel mobile layouts are visually validated.
