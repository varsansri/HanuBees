# The 4 Virality Levers — read before making ANY content

Founder's framework (2026-06-09). On social, **3 things always get views, + 1 cheat code**.
Every single post we make should be built on at least ONE of these. If a post isn't
funny, isn't riding a trend, isn't a reaction, and isn't modeled on a proven viral
hook — it will not get views. No exceptions.

Our brand = **faceless business-niche page** (small/local business growth), value-first,
with ONE quiet Hanubees footer at the end. So each lever below is translated into how
WE use it in the business niche.

---

## 1. FUNNY
Memes, humor, anything that makes people laugh. The fastest scroll-stopper + most shared.
- **For us:** business/owner-life memes ("POV: a customer asks if you're open... 3 days
  of silence"), relatable small-biz pain humor, funny truths about running a shop,
  meme-format carousels. Humor first, lesson second, footer last.

## 2. TREND
NOT just a dance or a video sound. A trend can be a **topic**, a piece of **news that
just dropped**, a hot subject people are already talking about.
- **For us:** react to fresh business/economy/AI/marketing news, a viral business story,
  a trending topic small owners care about (a new platform fee, an AI tool, a holiday/
  seasonal surge). Ride attention that already exists. → needs a light "what's trending"
  feed/check before posting.

## 3. REACTION
Two ways:
  (a) Take a **popular video** and react to it with something **valuable enough** to
      justify the steal of attention.
  (b) Take the **intro/premise** of a popular piece and give **your perspective** — your
      tip, your angle, your trick on it.
- **For us:** "This blew up saying X about business — here's what they got right/wrong
  for a small shop," or take a viral business clip's hook and add our practical tip.

## 4. COPY A PROVEN VIRAL POST/HOOK → RESTRUCTURE FOR OUR NICHE  *(the cheat code)*
Don't invent virality. Find a post/hook that **already** went viral, keep its structure,
swap in our business-niche content. Proven skeleton + our substance.
- **For us:** mine viral hooks (business, motivation, life-advice pages), keep the format,
  rewrite the body for small-business owners. The single highest-odds lever.

---

## Rule of thumb when generating content
Before building a post, pick its lever: **Funny · Trend · Reaction · Proven-hook copy.**
Tag each script with which lever it uses. The learning loop should track which lever
wins and bias the queue toward it. Value/tips alone (no lever) = low ceiling — always
wrap the value inside one of the 4.
