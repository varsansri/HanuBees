4. CONTENT METHOD — LOCKED (full detail in CONTENT_METHOD.md)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Topic universe: "TOP 10 of <niche> THIS YEAR" — wealthiest companies, wealthiest
people, footballers, cars, female influencers, music artists, influencers,
business creators, etc. Always current-year framing.

The ONLY format right now = DIFFERENCE / COMPARISON. (Q&A and raw-numbers are
allowed flavors, but DIFFERENCES win views, so that is the single focus.)

Per-post narrative (the "story" that continues as you swipe):
  -> pick a TOP 10 of a niche
  -> COMPARE it against something relevant
  -> frame slide 1 as a QUESTION (the difference)
  -> slides 2-3 reveal the COMPARISON / DIFFERENCE in HARD REAL NUMBERS
  -> resolve with the answer/solution
  -> slide 4 = the fixed Hanubees agency card (see sec 6)

TRUTH ONLY: no fake stats ever. LLM drafts the facts, a verification pass
confirms the numbers before anything renders. (Default — founder may override.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SLIDE ANATOMY — 4 slides per post (LOCKED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Canvas: 1080 x 1080 (Threads carousel). Reel = same slides re-rendered 1080x1920.

Slides 1-3 (the content slides) composition, back-to-front:
  (a) BACKGROUND: dark #121212 + premium gradient.
  (b) LOGO LAYER (behind): the subject's brand logo, BACKGROUND REMOVED, large,
      sitting behind the person.
  (c) PERSON LAYER (front): a DIFFERENT photo of the subject on each slide
      (cut-out / foreground). Last 4 years only — fresh media.
  (d) BLACK GRADIENT: bottom 30% of the canvas, black -> transparent going up.
  (e) TEXT: lives ONLY inside that bottom-30% band. Perfectly aligned, clearly
      visible, Space Grotesk, brand colors. This carries the Q&A / difference.
  (f) WATERMARKS: bee.png logo + "hanubees.com" on every slide.

Slide 4 (FIXED agency card — identical role on every post):
  - Hanubees bee PNG placed BIGGER (hero).
  - Copy (final wording TBD with founder, working draft):
    "We're a marketing agency producing a high volume of posts and videos
     to dominate social media."
  - Same font + colors. This slide is mandatory on EVERY post.

NON-NEGOTIABLE: every single image is individually PLANNED (layout/alignment/
crop) THEN created. No batch-stamping ugly templates. Craft each one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. MEDIA SCRAPING PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each subject in a post:
  - Pull photos from >= 5 different sources (Wikimedia Commons, + others TBD).
  - FILTER: only images from the LAST 4 YEARS (no dated/funny old photos).
  - Collect as MANY assets as possible so each slide uses a different image.
  - Grab the brand LOGO; prefer already-transparent Wikimedia PNG/SVG; otherwise
    background-remove it locally (rembg / U2Net — free, unlimited). Default.
  - Cut out the person where needed for the foreground layer.
Store scraped raw assets under assets/scraped/<subject>/.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. UNIQUENESS (hard rule)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every post is 100% unique to ONE account/platform. The same subject/info is
never reused on a second account. Reuse the shared Supabase "claim" DB
(claim.js pattern from Media Army) so two accounts never ship the same thing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. PLATFORM ROUTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THREADS  -> IMAGE carousel (the 4 slides as-is).
IG / TIKTOK / YOUTUBE -> VIDEO reel built FROM the same 4 slides (+ music),
                         1080x1920, via the ffmpeg make-reel pipeline.
Image posts are ONLY for Threads. Other platforms always get the video.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. LIFECYCLE / CLEANUP  (build LATER — not now)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each image is created once. After a post is posted via the scheduler, its PNG is
DELETED (post-then-delete, programmed). Deferred until after posters look right.
