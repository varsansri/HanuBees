## EXAMPLE CONCEPTS (proof of the content — these are the worked-problem format)

### #1 — The Funnel (Conversion Rate)
HOOK: "50 approaches. 0 girlfriends. Here's the math."
GIVEN: Reach R = 50/mo. Stages: Approach→Convo 40%, Convo→Number 25%, Number→Date 30%, Date→GF 20%.
FORMULA: G = R × (c1·c2·c3·c4)
SOLVE: c = 0.40·0.25·0.30·0.20 = 0.006 → G = 50 × 0.006 = 0.3
ANSWER: You don't need MORE reach. Find your lowest cn and fix THAT stage. (Marketing = fix the leak.)

### #2 — Scarcity (Supply & Demand)
HOOK: "Why replying in 0.2 seconds kills attraction."
GIVEN: You reply 30×/day → Supply S high.
FORMULA: Perceived Value V = Demand D / Supply S
SOLVE: S → ∞  ⇒  V → 0
ANSWER: Lower the supply of your attention and V rises. Scarcity = price. (Marketing 101.)

### #3 — CAC vs LTV (cut your losses)
HOOK: "The ROI on chasing her for 6 months."
GIVEN: CAC = 180 days of effort. Interest signals ≈ 0 → LTV ≈ 0.
FORMULA: ROI = (LTV − CAC) / CAC
SOLVE: ROI = (0 − 180)/180 = −100%
ANSWER: If interest ≈ 0, stop spending. Reallocate budget. (Don't pour spend into a dead lead.)

### #4 — A/B Testing (your opener)
HOOK: "Stop guessing your first line. Test it."
GIVEN: Opener A: 12 sent, 2 replies. Opener B: 12 sent, 6 replies.
FORMULA: response rate r = replies/sent
SOLVE: rA = 2/12 = 16.7%,  rB = 6/12 = 50%  → B wins by 3×
ANSWER: Ship the winner, kill the loser, test the next variable. (Marketing = test, don't guess.)

### #5 — Positioning (differentiation)
HOOK: "She has 200 'hey' texts. Here's how to not be #201."
GIVEN: Market = 200 identical messages. Differentiation Δ = 0 → you blend in.
FORMULA: P(noticed) ∝ Δ (distance from the average competitor)
ANSWER: Maximize Δ — say the thing no one else says. (Positioning = be different, not better.)
