# HOOK FORMAT — from "1,000 Viral Hooks (PBL)"

Source PDF: `docs/hooks/1000-viral-hooks.pdf` (63 pages, by Personal Brand Launch).
Hooks come ONLY from here. Write our hooks in THIS format.

## The format
A 2-column table:
- LEFT = a hook TEMPLATE with `(insert X)` fill-in-the-blank slots, grouped by
  category (first column header seen: **EDUCATIONAL**).
- RIGHT = an INSPO HOOK link (the real reel/post it came from).

To use one: take a template, fill the `(insert ...)` slots with our top-10
subject + the comparison/difference, keep the structure intact.

## Templates that fit our DIFFERENCE / TOP-10 angle (examples from the PDF)
- "This is what (insert thing) looks like when you're (insert action). And this
  is what they look like when you're not (insert action)."
- "(insert trait) Guy vs (insert trait) Guy."
- "Here's the difference between (insert title), (insert title), and (insert title)."
- "(insert industry) tier list for (insert year)."
- "Ranking all the most popular (insert noun), so I can rank them from worst to best."
- "(insert trait) responsible ... here are 3 things ..." (numbered-difference style)
- "This is the same exact (insert thing) but the first is/got (insert result) and
  the second is/got X."
- "(Insert last year) (insert noun), (insert current year) (insert noun)."  (then-vs-now)
- "Did you know that this, this, this, and this get (insert dream result)."

## Rule
Do NOT invent hooks from scratch. Pull a template from the PDF, adapt the slots
to the top-10 subject + its comparison, and frame slide 1 as the question.
