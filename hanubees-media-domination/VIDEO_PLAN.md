# Flagship build: Concept #1 "The Funnel". Data-driven so all 13 concepts plug in.
# Engine: Remotion (reuse hanubees-marketing-team/video). Target: amaze in <1s, teach in <25s.

================================================================================
0. THE BIG IDEA (why this video format wins)
================================================================================
Every post is a MATH PROOF that a dating truth is really a marketing law.
We film it like a live-solved problem: THEOREM → GIVEN → VISUAL MODEL → FORMULA →
TWIST → Q.E.D. The viewer feels they "did the math" with us, so the lesson sticks.
The hook ("50 approaches. 0 girlfriends.") buys the first second; the SOLVE keeps
them; the Q.E.D. line is the screenshot/share moment.

Format name: THE PROOF (composition id = "ProofReel").
Canvas: 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps. **15.0s = 450 frames** (tight, cinematic). Reuses theme.ts.
Audio: the cleared no-copyright "INTERSTELLAR Heart" track (every IG post). Threads = silent.

CINEMATIC TREATMENT (NEW — "treat it like a film"):
  • ONE continuous virtual CAMERA across all cuts — dolly in on the hook, pull back to
    reveal the funnel floating in 3D space, slow orbit during the solve, push into Q.E.D.
    Motion is eased + weighty (camera has mass), never linear. This is what sells "film".
  • 3D DEPTH: scene elements live on separate Z-planes (bg field / mid model / fg type).
    Parallax + perspective so camera moves feel volumetric, not flat.
  • DEPTH-OF-FIELD BLUR: whatever the camera isn't focused on is blurred (CSS blur driven
    by z-distance). Focus PULLS between planes on cuts (rack-focus) — pure film language.
  • WARM BROWN/AMBER GRADE ("Interstellar" look): deep brown→black base, amber key light,
    floating dust motes/bokeh, soft vignette, subtle film grain + faint chromatic edge.
    Brand yellow #FFBE00 becomes the warm key; greens cool the shadows. NOT flat dark.
Aesthetic: cinematic brown/amber grade + editorial-halftone garnish (from inspo-02) IN
MOTION — hairlines draw on, mono corner metadata, a barcode builds, dust + halftone field.

1. WHAT MAKES IT "AMAZE" (the 3 signature moments)
================================================================================
A) THE DRAINING FUNNEL (centerpiece, scene 3)
   50 glowing dots pour into a funnel. At each stage a % survives; the rest visibly
   LEAK out the sides and fall away (gravity + fade). By the bottom only ~0.3 of a dot
   remains, pulsing alone. You literally watch 50 become 0. Nobody scrolls past that.

B) THE SELF-WRITING FORMULA (scene 4)
   G = R × (c1·c2·c3·c4) draws itself stroke-by-stroke (SVG dash). Then the real numbers
   FLY UP from the funnel stages and slot into the variables. Multiply chain animates
   down to 0.006, then ×50 snaps to a giant 0.3. Math that moves = "high value" proof.

C) THE FIX (scene 5 twist)
   We double reach (50→100): the giant number only crawls to 0.6 — still < 1. Beat.
   Then the WEAKEST stage glows red and we drag it 20%→50%: the number SLAMS to 0.75.
   Visual, undeniable: fix the leak, not the traffic. This is the marketing payload.

2. SCENE-BY-SCENE BEAT SHEET (frame-accurate @ 30fps — 15s / 450f, cinematic)
================================================================================
Total 450f / 15.0s. 6 beats. The CAMERA never cuts hard — it MOVES between framings
(dolly/orbit/rack-focus); "cuts" are really focus pulls + camera repositions. Warm
brown/amber grade + dust + vignette + grain run on EVERY beat (one global layer).

CAMERA PATH (continuous, eased): push-in (S0) → big pull-back reveal (S1→S2) →
slow left orbit around the funnel (S2) → settle + rack-focus to formula plane (S3) →
small push on the twist number (S4) → final push through to the Q.E.D. slate (S5).

── S0  COLD OPEN HOOK ──────────────────────────  f0–45  (1.5s)   CAMERA: slow push-in
  • Brown-black void, amber rim light, dust motes drifting in deep focus. Sub-bass swell.
  • Mono types top-left: "THEOREM 01 / CONVERSION".
  • f6: "50 approaches." slams in on the FG plane (scale 0.3→1 spring), sharp focus.
  • f20: "0 girlfriends." drops below in cooled green, 12px impact shake.
  • Camera eases forward the whole time (parallax on dust). DOF blur on the bg field.
  HOOK = the 50-vs-0 contradiction, in a frame that already looks like a film.

── S1  THE REFRAME (dating → marketing) ────────  f45–110  (2.2s)  CAMERA: pull back
  • Camera dollies BACK; the hook recedes onto a mid Z-plane and softens (rack-focus).
  • Word-morph on the focused plane: "GIRLFRIEND"→"CUSTOMER", "APPROACH"→"LEAD" (per-char
    blur+slide, 12f each, only 2 morphs now — tighter for 15s).
  • f95: resolves "It's a FUNNEL. Funnels leak." (warm yellow "FUNNEL") as the pull-back
    ends and the empty 3D funnel fades up behind, out of focus.

── S2  THE DRAINING FUNNEL (signature A) ───────  f110–245  (4.5s)  CAMERA: slow orbit
  • Focus pulls to the funnel plane. Camera orbits left ~12° — the funnel reads as a real
    3D object (perspective rings, depth shading). This beat gets the MOST screen time.
  • Given values fly in as labels ON the funnel stages (R=50; c₁40 c₂25 c₃30 c₄20%);
    the 20% ring glows faintly (weak-link foreshadow). No separate "given" scene — it
    builds INTO the model (saves time for 15s).
  • f150: 50 amber dots pour into the mouth (gravity). At each of 4 rings only cₙ% pass;
    the rest spray sideways, tumble out of focus and fall away = THE LEAK. Survivor
    counter ticks 50 → 20 → 5 → 1.5 → 0.3. Stage tags flash "−60 −75 −70 −80%".
  • f230: one dim dot pulses alone at the spout → "0.3 / month". Let it hurt (0.4s hold).
  THE MONEY SHOT. The orbit + DOF makes the leak feel cinematic, not chart-y.

── S3  FORMULA WRITES ITSELF (signature B) ─────  f245–330  (2.8s)  CAMERA: rack-focus
  • Funnel blurs to a bokeh ghost on a back plane; focus snaps to the formula plane.
  • f245: "G = R × (c₁·c₂·c₃·c₄)" draws stroke-by-stroke (SVG dashoffset), 28f.
  • f285: the four % values FLY from the blurred funnel into the parentheses → (0.40·0.25·
    0.30·0.20). Collapses → "= 0.006" → "G = 50 × 0.006" → MEGA "0.3" pops (spring, shake 8).
  • Amber key flares on the answer. Mono: "the math agrees with the funnel."

── S4  THE TWIST: fix the leak, not the reach ──  f330–405  (2.5s)  CAMERA: push on number
  • f330: "Approach MORE? R = 100?" Camera pushes slightly on the giant number.
  • f345: R 50→100; G only crawls 0.3→0.6 — still < 1. Red "STILL SINGLE" stamp thumps.
  • f375: weakest ring (c₄=20%) flares red, a slider drags 20%→50%; G SLAMS 0.3→0.75 (green
    spring). Caption locks: "Reach didn't fix it. The WEAK STAGE did."
  THE LESSON, shown not told.

── S5  Q.E.D. + BRAND (the screenshot line) ────  f405–450  (1.5s)  CAMERA: push through
  • Camera pushes through to a near-black slate (deepest brown). Mono "Q.E.D." top.
  • Two stacked truths:
        MARKETING:  "Fix your worst step — not your traffic."
        DATING:     "Stop approaching more. Fix where you lose them."
  • Barcode builds L→R; bee.png + "hanubees.com · daily proofs" settle in as the Zimmer
    swell peaks. (CTA folded into Q.E.D. — no separate scene at 15s.)
  THIS is the share/caption moment.

3. PROPS SCHEMA (data-driven → all 13 concepts reuse this one component)
================================================================================
type ProofReelProps = {
  id: string;                 // "p01"
  theorem: string;            // "CONVERSION"
  hookTop: string;            // "50 approaches."
  hookBottom: string;         // "0 girlfriends."
  reframe: {                  // S1 morph pairs
    pairs: [string, string][];// [["GIRLFRIEND","CUSTOMER"],["APPROACH","LEAD"], ...]
    resolve: string;          // "It's a FUNNEL. And funnels leak."
  };
  given: { sym: string; label: string; value: string; weak?: boolean }[];
  model:                      // which visual engine S3 uses
    | { kind: "funnel"; reach: number; stages: number[] /* [0.40,0.25,0.30,0.20] */ }
    | { kind: "ratio"; supply: number; demand: number }      // scarcity (#2)
    | { kind: "bars"; a: number; b: number }                 // A/B test (#4)
    | { kind: "curve"; fn: "compound" | "sqrt" | "decay" }   // #7 #8
    | { kind: "bayes"; prior: number; signal: number };      // #12
  formula: { tex: string; subs: string[]; steps: string[]; answer: string };
  twist: { question: string; before: string; after: string; lesson: string };
  qed: { marketing: string; dating: string };
  emotion: Emotion;           // drives accent shade only (music is fixed track)
};
NOTE: model.kind is the ONE thing that swaps per concept. Funnel/ratio/bars/curve/bayes
are 5 small visual modules; the 13 concepts map onto them (most reuse 2–3 of them).

4. VISUAL SYSTEM (grounded in theme.ts — what we keep / add)
================================================================================
KEEP (from existing engine):
  • Font Space Grotesk (loaded once, single delayRender, 90s timeout — CI-safe).
  • COLORS bg/bg2/yellow/green/fg/muted. TYPE scale (mega 380 … small 30).
  • Rise spring helper, SPRING snappy/soft, persistent bee + "@hanubees" footer.
  • <Audio> bed pattern (swap the 3 CC beds for the one Interstellar track).
ADD (new, small, reusable):
  • <Halftone> — fixed dot-grid <pattern> at 5–8% opacity, slow breathe (bg on every scene).
  • <Hairline> — a rule that draws L→R via width interpolate (the editorial garnish).
  • <MonoLabel> — letter-spaced caps micro-text for corner metadata + stage labels.
  • <Barcode> — vertical bars that build in sequence (S6/S7 callback to static posts).
  • <DrawPath> — wraps any SVG path with stroke-dashoffset reveal (funnel + formula).
  • <DotFlow> — the particle system for the funnel (spawn, gravity, leak, survivor count).
  • <WordMorph> — per-character blur+slide swap of word A → word B (reframe scene).
CINEMATIC ADDS (the "film" layer):
  • <Camera> — wraps a scene in a moving virtual camera. Drives a parent transform:
    perspective(1200px) + translateZ/translateX/Y + rotateY(orbit), all eased (not linear),
    so child Z-planes parallax. One <Camera> path spans the whole 450f (see §2 camera path).
  • <Zplane depth={n}> — places content at a Z-distance; auto-applies DOF blur = f(|focus−n|)
    so the camera's focus plane is sharp and others soften. Rack-focus = animate `focus`.
  • <Grade> — global top layer: brown→black radial base, amber light wash (screen blend),
    vignette, ~4% film grain (animated noise), faint chromatic aberration on edges.
  • <Dust> — slow-drifting bokeh motes on 2 depth planes (the Interstellar "dust" feel).
MOTION RULES: nothing static >0.8s; every number arrives by spring; leaks use ease-in
gravity; the answer lands with a scale-pop + shake; CAMERA always has weight (ease, never
linear) and is always subtly moving. Read-in-1s contrast even through the grade.

3D APPROACH (decision in §11): default = **CSS pseudo-3D** (perspective + transforms +
blur) — light, fast, CI/ARM-safe, gives 90% of the film feel for type/particles. Option B =
**@remotion/three** (real Three.js camera + true depth) for genuine volumetric 3D, heavier
to render headless on ARM. Recommend CSS-3D for v1 (ship fast), Three.js as a v2 upgrade.

7. HOW THE 13 CONCEPTS MAP (so this isn't a one-off)
================================================================================
  #1 Funnel ........... model=funnel        (built first, flagship)
  #2 Scarcity ......... model=ratio (V=D/S, supply→∞ shrinks V to 0)
  #3 ROI .............. model=bars (cost vs return, negative fill)
  #4 A/B test ......... model=bars (A 17% vs B 50%, 3× winner)
  #5 Positioning ...... model=funnel-ish "blend-in" field (200 identical dots + 1 Δ dot)
  #6 Expected value ... model=ratio (tiny cost vs huge upside, +EV bar)
  #7 Compound ......... model=curve fn=compound (1.01^90 vs spike decay)
  #8 Diminishing ...... model=curve fn=sqrt (text 1 vs text 49)
  #9 Loss aversion .... model=bars (pain 2× joy)
  #10 80/20 ........... model=bars (2 of 10 behaviors = 80%)
  #11 Signal/noise .... model=ratio (SNR up when noise cut)
  #12 Bayes ........... model=bayes (prior 30% → posterior 65%)
  #13 Opportunity cost  model=bars (wrong lead vs best alternative)
Every concept keeps S0/S1/S2/S4/S6/S7 identical in STRUCTURE; only S3 (model) + the
numbers change. That's how we hit volume without it feeling repetitive — same proof
ritual, totally different math each time.
