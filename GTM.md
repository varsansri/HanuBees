## A. VIRAL MARKETING — fear/FOMO shorts & reels (target: Melbourne SMBs)

**Strategy:** break attention with emotion (panic/FOMO), then deliver the brand turn.
Dominate ONE city (Melbourne), use it as proof, move to the next. Real local footage
so it doesn't feel synthetic.

**Hard rules (protect the brand + stay legal):**
- **No fabricated "news" and no false claims** (e.g. "your competitor already uses this"
  when untrue). In Australia that's misleading conduct under Australian Consumer Law (ACCC).
  True FOMO hits harder anyway.
- **Don't repost identifiable strangers' footage** (Snapmap etc.) in ads — copyright +
  privacy + Snapchat ToS. Scout locations there, then film it yourself / license / pay UGC creators.
- Style it "ALERT/breaking" but don't impersonate a real news channel.

**Hook formula:** `[EMOTION WORD] + [true threat] -> [brand turn] -> [CTA]`

**12 ready hooks (emotion word first):**
1. WARNING. People stopped Googling — they ask AI "who does this near me?" If your shop isn't in it, you don't exist.
2. UH OH. A customer just asked AI for the best [cafe/plumber] in Melbourne. It never mentioned you.
3. WHILE YOU SLEPT. Someone messaged your business at 1am. You replied at 9am. They'd already booked someone else.
4. CAUTION. "I'll get back to you" is costing you 9 of 10 enquiries.
5. HEADS UP. Your competitor's AI answers every question instantly. Yours sends people to voicemail.
6. QUIETLY. Shops on this street are becoming "askable" by AI. The ones that aren't are going invisible.
7. BAD NEWS. Your website has the answer on page 3. Nobody reads page 3. The AI does — if you let it.
8. PANIC? You don't need a website, ads, or followers anymore. You need an agent that answers for you.
9. NOBODY TOLD YOU. Customers compare 5 businesses in one chat. You're only in it if you're on Hanubees.
10. TICK TOCK. First business in your suburb with an AI receptionist eats the enquiries. Second gets leftovers. (← sharpest: true, local, zero-sum)
11. PSA. You're not losing to a better shop. You're losing to a faster reply.
12. THEY KNEW. The smart ones already gave their business a 24/7 AI. For free.

**Brand turn (end every clip):** "Give your business its own AI. It answers customers while you sleep. Free to start → Hanubees."

**Reel structure (8–10s vertical):** 0–1.5s black + ALERT sting + emotion word · 1.5–4s real Melbourne b-roll + threat line · 4–7s phone showing AI instantly answering · 7–10s logo + "Be the first in your suburb" + Free.

**Locations that read as real Melbourne:** Degraves St / Centre Place, Lygon St (Carlton), Brunswick & Gertrude St (Fitzroy), Chapel St (South Yarra), Queen Victoria Market, South Melbourne Market, Acland St (St Kilda).

**Shot types:** "we'll get back to you"/closed sign · empty vs busy counter · phone lighting up unanswered · hands typing into AI chat, instant reply · owner asleep, phone pinging · Google search with no good answer → AI gives the exact answer.

**Captions:** "Your competitor doesn't sleep anymore. Free → Hanubees. #melbournesmallbusiness" · "POV: a customer asked AI for the best in Melbourne and your shop wasn't in the chat."

## B. SALES / ONBOARDING — manual founder calls (target: India / Coimbatore SMBs)

**The move:** founder personally calls single-owner service businesses (plumber, tuition,
beautician, pet care, etc.), learns about them, sets up their FREE profile + agent, makes
them aware they can use it too. Honest, legal, high-converting. Call in **Tamil/local language**.

**NOT this (illegal — do not do):** pre-recorded/robocall blasts, looping recorded audio,
or hiding/spoofing the caller's number/country. Illegal (ACMA in AU; TRAI/DND in India),
gets carrier-blocked, and brands you as a scam. Manual genuine calls only; honor "not interested";
don't auto-dial at mass scale.

**Honesty rule:** don't run the "fake customer who wants to buy" routine then surprise-pivot —
owners feel tricked on the reveal and you lose them. Lead with the genuine free-listing reason.

**Call flow (~3 min):**
1. **Open (10s, permission):** "Hi, I'm [name] from Hanubees. I help local Coimbatore businesses get found when people search online — I want to list yours FREE. Can I ask 4–5 quick questions?"
2. **Discover = collect data:** services? areas covered? typical charges? timings? what makes you better than others nearby (USP)? what do customers ask most? best contact number?
3. **Pitch:** "I'll create a free profile + an AI that answers customers 24/7 — so when someone searches 'plumber near me' or 'maths tuition in Gandhipuram', you show up and it answers for you."
4. **Consent + awareness:** "Shall I set it up with what you told me? You'll get a WhatsApp link — and you can update it anytime by just calling and speaking, no typing."
5. **Close / recall hook:** "Done — when someone searches your service, your profile answers. I'll WhatsApp the link + a screenshot." Later recall: "you got 3 enquiries this week — want to add prices so it converts better?"

**Data checklist (= a complete agent):** name · category · area/city · phone · hours · services ·
pricing · USP (what's special) · common questions · current offer.

**Objections to prep:** "I'm too busy" (takes 2 min, I do the work) · "is it really free" (yes,
free to start) · "I don't use apps" (you don't need to — just speak, I set it up).

**Product gap this needs:** a **Call-Onboarding tool** — a page/voice box where the founder
enters/speaks a business's answers DURING the call and it instantly creates their account +
agent + listings and returns the shareable @handle.B link to WhatsApp. (Self-serve claim is
still parked; this admin-create flow is the next build to make phone onboarding 60 seconds.)
